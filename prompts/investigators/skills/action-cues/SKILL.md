# A finite action vocabulary, not a prescribed script

The project uses 44 possible action names. The family references below cover every
name. Questions may offer paraphrases; use their supplied letters and wording for
answers. A vocabulary entry does not establish that an activity occurred.

These recordings are acted. Actions can be brief, incomplete, exaggerated, awkward,
or performed without a realistic outcome. A single clear diagnostic gesture can be
more useful than demanding real-world task completion. Washing dishes need not show
water; checking time need not reveal a watch face. Conversely, acting does not
justify inventing an object or treating any vague gesture as the intended action.
Prefer the observed interaction sequence and discriminate against the strongest
alternative. Retain uncertainty when two labels remain plausible.

The cues below describe physical patterns to inspect. They are not frequency
estimates or universal rules that override the visible context. A family reference
may identify a clear gesture as enough to support an acted action without showing
its real-world outcome; the gesture itself still needs to be visible and distinguished
from the strongest alternative. Only the objects skill reports audited training statistics.
Inspect source context and action onset when a cue is hidden; missing fine detail
is weaker counterevidence than a clearly conflicting motion.

Load the relevant family, not the whole catalog:

- [personal-care](references/personal-care.md): wash face, brush teeth, comb hair,
  undress, wipe hands, get dressed, take medicine, check body temperature, self-massage.
- [food-and-drink](references/food-and-drink.md): drink, eat, grab utensils, pour,
  stir, peel fruit, peel fruit with a knife.
- [household](references/household.md): sweep, mop, wash dishes, wipe surfaces,
  fold clothes, throw away rubbish, open a cabinet to get things.
- [desk-and-devices](references/desk-and-devices.md): type, write, call, check time,
  read, turn a page, take selfie, play board games, watch TV, use phone,
  listen with headphones.
- [body-movement](references/body-movement.md): walk, lunges, sit down, lie down,
  stand up, stretch, jumping jacks, squats, run, balance on one leg.
